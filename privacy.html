Privacy policy

Personal GWS CLI is a personal, single-user integration. It accesses Gmail messages and drafts through Google APIs when authorized by the account owner, to search and read mail and prepare drafts at the owner's request. The Gmail compose permission can also send drafts; sending requires the owner's authorization.

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Email content and outputs may be processed by the owner's chosen AI provider and retained in the local assistant's session records or files as needed to perform requested work. The integration does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

The account owner may revoke access at any time through Google Account third-party connections and may remove local credentials and saved assistant data.

Contact: the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
